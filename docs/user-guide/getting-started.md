# Getting Started with Video Import

Welcome! This guide will get you up and running with the Video Import feature in just a few minutes.

## What You Can Do

The Video Import feature transforms long-form videos into engaging short-form content by:

- 🎬 **Importing from popular platforms** - YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok
- 🤖 **AI-powered highlight detection** - Automatically finds the best moments
- 🎵 **Adding background music** - Choose from various moods and styles
- 📱 **Smart orientation cropping** - Convert to portrait, landscape, or square
- 🌍 **Multi-language support** - Transcribe and translate content
- ⚡ **Batch processing** - Import multiple videos at once

## Quick Start (5 Minutes)

### Step 1: Access the Import Feature
1. Navigate to your dashboard
2. Click on "Import Video" in the navigation menu
3. You'll see a 3-step import wizard

### Step 2: Enter Video URL
1. Copy any supported video URL (YouTube, Instagram, etc.)
2. Paste it into the URL input field
3. The system will automatically detect the platform
4. Click "Next" to proceed

### Step 3: Configure Settings
Choose your preferences:
- **Target Language**: The language for subtitles/transcription
- **Background Music**: Select a mood (Upbeat, Calm, Dramatic, etc.)
- **Video Orientation**: Portrait (TikTok/Reels), Landscape (YouTube), or Square (Instagram)
- **AI Highlights**: Enable automatic highlight detection
- **Overlay**: Optional branding or subscribe buttons

### Step 4: Start Import
1. Review your settings
2. Click "Start Import"
3. Watch the real-time progress
4. Your processed videos will appear in your library

## Supported Platforms

| Platform | URL Examples | Notes |
|----------|--------------|-------|
| **YouTube** | `https://youtube.com/watch?v=...` | Full videos, shorts, live streams |
| **Instagram** | `https://instagram.com/p/...` | Posts, Reels, IGTV |
| **Facebook** | `https://facebook.com/watch/...` | Public videos only |
| **TikTok** | `https://tiktok.com/@user/video/...` | Public videos |
| **Direct URLs** | `https://example.com/video.mp4` | Direct video file links |

## What Happens During Import?

1. **Download** - The system downloads your video
2. **Transcription** - Audio is transcribed to text
3. **Analysis** - AI analyzes content for highlights
4. **Processing** - Video is cut into segments and optimized
5. **Rendering** - Final videos are created with your settings

## Processing Time

- **Short videos (< 5 min)**: 2-5 minutes
- **Medium videos (5-20 min)**: 5-15 minutes  
- **Long videos (20-60 min)**: 15-30 minutes

Times vary based on video complexity and current system load.

## Common First-Time Issues

**❌ "Invalid URL" error**
- Ensure the video is publicly accessible
- Check that the URL is complete and correct

**❌ "Processing failed" error**  
- The video might be too long (max 60 minutes)
- Check your internet connection
- Try a different video to test

**❌ Slow processing**
- Processing time varies with video length
- High-resolution videos take longer
- System load can affect speed
