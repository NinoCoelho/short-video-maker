# Import Settings Guide

This comprehensive guide explains every setting available in the video import feature, helping you optimize your results for different content types and target platforms.

## Settings Overview

The import settings are organized into logical sections:
- **Language & Transcription** - Speech processing and translation
- **Background Music** - Audio enhancement options  
- **Video Orientation** - Aspect ratio and cropping
- **AI Features** - Automated content analysis
- **Overlay Options** - Branding and visual elements
- **Advanced Settings** - Fine-tuning for specific needs

## Language & Transcription Settings

### Target Language Selection

**Purpose**: Determines the language for final subtitles and content analysis

**Options:**
- **Portuguese (pt)** - Primary language, best accuracy
- **English (en)** - Excellent support, most content
- **Spanish (es)** - Good support, growing library
- **French (fr)** - European content support
- **German (de)** - Technical content friendly
- **Italian (it)** - Romance language support
- **Japanese (ja)** - Asian content support
- **Korean (ko)** - K-pop and Korean content
- **Chinese (zh)** - Simplified Chinese support

**How it Works:**
1. **Source Detection** - AI identifies original video language
2. **Transcription** - Speech converted to text in original language
3. **Translation** - Content translated to target language (if different)
4. **Subtitle Generation** - Translated text becomes subtitles
5. **Content Analysis** - AI analysis performed in target language

**Best Practices:**
- **Keep original language** when possible for best accuracy
- **Translate only when necessary** for your target audience
- **Consider audience location** when choosing target language
- **Test translation quality** with shorter videos first

### Transcription Quality Options

**Automatic (Recommended)**
- Chooses best model based on content
- Balances accuracy with processing speed
- Handles multiple speakers well
- Good for most content types

**High Accuracy**
- Uses most advanced transcription models
- Slower processing but better results
- Best for: Educational content, interviews
- Recommended for: Professional content

**Fast Processing**
- Prioritizes speed over accuracy
- Good for: Casual content, testing
- May miss nuances in speech
- Best for: High-volume processing

### Language-Specific Considerations

**English Content:**
- Highest transcription accuracy (95%+)
- Best AI analysis quality
- Most music compatibility
- Fastest processing times

**Portuguese Content:**
- Native language support
- Excellent accuracy with Brazilian Portuguese
- Good European Portuguese support
- Optimized for local content

**Spanish Content:**
- Good accuracy for multiple dialects
- Mexico/Latin America optimized
- Spain Spanish also supported
- Growing content library

**Other Languages:**
- Accuracy varies by language
- Processing may be slower
- Some features may be limited
- Manual review recommended

**Advanced Mixing Options**

- **Speech Priority**: Music very quiet, focus on dialogue
- **Balanced**: Equal presence of music and speech
- **Dynamic**: Music volume changes with content energy

## Video Orientation Settings

### Aspect Ratio Options

**📱 Portrait (9:16)**
```
Dimensions: 1080x1920 pixels
Perfect for: TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts
Mobile optimization: Excellent
Viewing experience: Full-screen mobile

Smart cropping:
- Focuses on main subject (people, faces)
- Follows movement and action
- Maintains subject in frame
- Creates smooth camera movements

Best practices:
- Ensure main content is in center area
- Check that text/graphics remain visible
- Preview on mobile device
- Consider subject movement patterns
```

**🖥️ Landscape (16:9)**
```
Dimensions: 1920x1080 pixels
Perfect for: YouTube, Facebook, desktop viewing
Mobile optimization: Good (horizontal viewing)
Viewing experience: Traditional video format

Smart cropping:
- Preserves original framing when possible
- Optimizes for wide-screen content  
- Maintains cinematic composition
- Good for presentation-style content

Best practices:
- Ideal for educational content
- Great for screen recordings
- Maintains original director's intent
- Good for detailed visual content
```

**⬜ Square (1:1)**
```
Dimensions: 1080x1080 pixels  
Perfect for: Instagram feed, Facebook posts, LinkedIn
Mobile optimization: Very good
Viewing experience: Versatile, works everywhere

Smart cropping:
- Centers main subject
- Good compromise for all platforms
- Adapts well to different content types
- Balanced for mobile and desktop

Best practices:
- Universal compatibility
- Good for profile/headshot content
- Works well for product shots
- Safe choice when unsure
```

### Smart Cropping Technology

**How It Works:**
1. **Computer Vision Analysis**
   - Detects faces, people, objects
   - Identifies movement patterns
   - Analyzes scene composition
   - Tracks subject positioning

2. **Motion Prediction**
   - Predicts where subjects will move
   - Pre-positions crop area
   - Reduces jarring transitions
   - Maintains smooth motion

3. **Quality Assessment**  
   - Ensures important content stays visible
   - Maintains visual balance
   - Preserves key information
   - Avoids cutting off subjects

4. **Smooth Transitions**
   - Creates natural camera movements
   - Avoids sudden jumps
   - Maintains professional appearance
   - Reduces motion sickness

### Orientation-Specific Considerations

**Landscape to Portrait Conversion:**
- Most challenging transformation
- Requires careful subject tracking
- May lose peripheral content
- Best for single-subject videos

**Portrait to Landscape Conversion:**
- Usually involves adding background
- May reduce visual impact
- Good for preserving mobile-first content
- Often adds letterboxing

**Any to Square Conversion:**
- Good balance of preservation and adaptation
- Versatile for multiple platforms
- Minimal content loss
- Universal compatibility

## AI Features Settings

### Auto-Highlight Detection

**What It Analyzes:**
- **Speech Patterns** - Key phrases, emotional inflection, pace changes
- **Visual Content** - Scene transitions, action sequences, facial expressions
- **Audio Cues** - Music beats, sound effects, applause, laughter
- **Content Structure** - Topic changes, conclusions, reveals

**Scoring System:**
- **High Score (80-100)** - Viral potential, key moments, climaxes
- **Medium Score (60-80)** - Important content, good engagement
- **Low Score (0-60)** - Filler content, transitions, setup

**Customization Options:**
```
Sensitivity Settings:
- High Sensitivity: More segments, includes medium-score content
- Balanced: Recommended setting, good quality/quantity balance  
- Low Sensitivity: Fewer segments, only highest-scoring content

Content Type Hints:
- Educational: Focuses on key teaching moments
- Entertainment: Prioritizes funny/engaging moments
- Interview: Highlights best quotes and insights
- Review: Emphasizes conclusions and key points
```

**AI Model Selection:**
- **Standard Model** - Good for most content, fast processing
- **Advanced Model** - Better accuracy, slower processing
- **Content-Specific** - Optimized for educational, entertainment, etc.

## Overlay Settings

### Overlay Types

**None**
```
Clean video without any overlays
Perfect for: Professional content, minimalist style
Considerations: No branding or call-to-action
Best practices: Let content speak for itself
```

**Subscribe Button**
```
Animated call-to-action overlay
Positioning: Corner placement (customizable)
Timing: Appears at optimal moments during video
Animation: Subtle pulse or bounce effect
Customization: Text, colors, size adjustable

Best for: Growing social media presence
Effectiveness: Can increase follower conversion by 15-25%
Platforms: Most effective on YouTube, TikTok, Instagram
```

**Channel Logo/Watermark**
```
Your brand logo as semi-transparent overlay
Size: Small, non-intrusive
Position: Usually bottom corner
Opacity: 20-40% transparency
Format: PNG with transparent background preferred

Benefits: Brand recognition, copyright protection  
Considerations: Don't make it too prominent
Best practices: Keep consistent across all content
```

**Custom Image Overlay**
```
Upload your own overlay graphics
Supported formats: PNG, JPG, SVG
Transparency: Alpha channel supported
Positioning: Flexible placement options
Animation: Static or simple animations

Use cases:
- Event branding
- Special promotions  
- Website URLs
- Social handles
- Custom graphics
```

### Overlay Positioning

**Corner Placement:**
- **Top Left** - Traditional, doesn't interfere with most content
- **Top Right** - Good visibility, may conflict with platform UI
- **Bottom Left** - Safe choice, good for logos
- **Bottom Right** - Most common, high visibility

**Custom Positioning:**
- **Center** - High impact, use sparingly
- **Top Center** - Good for titles or event branding
- **Bottom Center** - Effective for call-to-actions
- **Edge Placement** - Subtle, professional appearance

### Overlay Timing

**Always Visible:**
- Consistent branding throughout video
- Good for watermarks and logos
- May reduce perceived video quality
- Best for professional/brand content

**Timed Appearance:**
- Appears at strategic moments
- Less intrusive than always-on
- AI determines optimal timing
- Good for subscribe buttons

**Manual Timing:**
- Specify exact appearance times
- Maximum control over placement
- Requires knowledge of content
- Best for specific campaigns

## Troubleshooting Settings

### Common Settings Issues

**Problem: Poor transcription quality**
```
Solutions:
- Specify correct source language
- Use "High Accuracy" transcription
- Check audio quality in original
- Try content with clearer speech
```

**Problem: Music doesn't match content**
```
Solutions:
- Review music mood descriptions
- Try "No Music" to preserve original
- Consider content type when choosing
- Preview before full processing
```

**Problem: Bad cropping results**
```
Solutions:
- Try different orientation
- Use content with clear main subject
- Check that important content is centered
- Consider manual cropping for complex scenes
```

**Problem: Segments too short/long**
```
Solutions:
- Adjust minimum/maximum duration settings
- Try different AI sensitivity levels
- Consider content type optimization
- Manual review and editing
```
